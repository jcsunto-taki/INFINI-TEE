Privacy and Terms

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how INFINI-TEE ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from the INFINI-TEE website (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or make a purchase on our Site, we collect certain information necessary to fulfill your order and optimize your shopping experience:

  • Order Information: When you purchase a T-shirt, we collect your name, billing address, shipping address, email address, phone number, and payment information.
  • Account Information: If you create an account with us, we collect your username and password.
  • Device & Usage Information: We automatically collect details about your device, web browser, IP address, time zone, cookies installed, and how you interact with the Site.

2. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use the collected personal information to:

  • Fulfill and ship your T-shirt orders, process payments, and issue invoices or order confirmations.
  • Communicate with you regarding your orders, shipping status, or customer support inquiries.
  • Screen orders for potential risk or fraud.
  • Send marketing communications, newsletters, or special promotions (only if you have opted in).
  • Improve and optimize our website experience and product catalog.

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We share your personal information with trusted third-party service providers to assist us in operating our shop and serving you:

  • E-commerce Platform: our e-commerce platform provider powers and operates our online store.
  • Payment Processors: our payment processors securely process your payments.
  • Fulfillment & Shipping Partners: our fulfillment and shipping partners print, pack, and deliver your orders.
  • Legal Compliance: We may share information to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or valid legal requests.
